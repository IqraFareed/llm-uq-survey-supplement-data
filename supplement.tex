\begin{abstract}
This document is the supplementary material to the survey article of the same title. It is not part of the article. It contains the per-paper literature matrix (Table~S1), the per-paper coding table (Table~S2), the update-search log (Table~S3), the disposition of the 126 distinct records returned by that search (Table~S4), and the cost and deployment table by method family (Table~S5). Section and table numbers cited as ``of the article'' refer to the article.
\end{abstract}

\noindent\emph{Notes on judgment calls for papers 43--45.} Badshah et al.\ (43) are coded open-ended because the judged answers are generated answers to open-domain questions, although the paper does not describe them as free-form and the judge itself outputs a binary verdict. \smallskip
\noindent\emph{Notes on judgment calls for papers 46--57.} Steyvers et al.\ (56) and Tao et al.\ (49) report calibration and discrimination together and are coded by their calibration result; Kapoor et al.\ (47) and Damani et al.\ (51) are coded calibration because their headline result is ECE.
